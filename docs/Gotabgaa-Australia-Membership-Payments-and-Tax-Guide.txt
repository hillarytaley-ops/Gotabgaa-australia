1. PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
How to collect membership fees directly (without Stripe/PayPal) and handle
GST, income tax, and record-keeping in Australia. Supports join.html portal
and admin Membership section (adjustable fees, CSV export).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. RECOMMENDED PAYMENT METHOD (AUSTRALIA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PRIMARY: PayID + bank transfer (EFT)
  - Money goes directly to the association's own bank account
  - PayID: instant (email, phone, or ABN linked to business account)
  - EFT: BSB + account number (backup; 1–2 business days)

OPTIONAL LATER: BPAY (requires biller setup with your bank)

MEMBER FLOW:
  Register on join.html
  → See fee + PayID/bank details + unique reference
  → Member pays from their bank
  → Treasurer checks bank account
  → Admin marks registration Paid
  → Send receipt to member

PAYMENT REFERENCE EXAMPLES:
  GAA-MEM-{registration-id}
  GAA-SURNAME-2026

BANK SETUP:
  - Business/community association account (not personal)
  - PayID linked to association
  - BSB + account for EFT
  - Optional BPAY biller later

NOT RECOMMENDED FIRST:
  - Stripe/PayPal (third-party; fees)
  - Card payments on own site (PCI, fraud, merchant complexity)
  - Cash only (hard to audit)
  - Personal bank account (governance/tax risk)

PHASES:
  Phase 1: PayID + EFT, unique reference, admin mark paid, CSV export
  Phase 2: BPAY, payment instruction emails, receipt upload
  Phase 3: Bank merchant/eftpos only if cards essential

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SECURITY & GOVERNANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Publish bank details only on official join.html / admin content
  - Dedicated membership bank account
  - Never pay to personal accounts (unless PayID is association-registered)
  - Ledger: ID, name, amount, date, method, who confirmed
  - Monthly bank reconciliation

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. TAX OVERVIEW (AUSTRALIA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GST:
  Under $75k turnover     → GST registration usually optional
  $75k+ turnover          → Generally must register; lodge BAS
  ACNC charity            → Some fees may be GST-free (confirm)
  Not GST-registered      → Don't charge GST; can't claim GST on expenses
  GST-registered          → May need to show GST on fees (confirm)

INCOME TAX:
  - NFP ≠ automatic zero tax
  - Small associations: may have minimal liability
  - ACNC charities: may be income tax exempt if conditions met
  - Non-charity: tax on some non-member income possible

ABN: Required for association bank account, receipts, PayID, transparency

DGR: Membership fees usually NOT tax-deductible; donations may differ
